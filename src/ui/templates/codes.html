Finding codes

Codes are stable identifiers. Prefix names the domain (IN- inventory, AR- arithmetic, VN- vendor, and so on); severity carries the routing weight. Source: docs/exception-taxonomy.md.

What each severity means for the decision

CRITICAL Cannot be paid under any reading. The §2.2 guardrail forbids auto-approve — the system overrides an APPROVE to ESCALATE.
HIGH Substantive defect. Requires human judgment.
MEDIUM Anomaly worth surfacing to the Adjudicator.
LOW / INFO Noted for the record; rarely decision-relevant on its own.
Guidance, not a mechanical rule. Verbatim from docs/exception-taxonomy.md.
